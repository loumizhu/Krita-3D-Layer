📦 Krita 3D Layer — User Manual

Krita 3D Layer is an interactive 3D reference viewer, perspective grid generator, and canvas renderer built natively for Krita. It allows digital artists, illustrators, and comic creators to place 3D models and mathematically accurate perspective grids directly onto Krita paint layers.

🌍 Multi-Platform Compatibility

Krita 3D Layer is 100% Cross-Platform! It works seamlessly on Windows, Linux, and macOS. It is built entirely using standard Python 3 and PyQt5 (bundled inside Krita) without any platform-dependent binaries or external C++ libraries.

🚀 Quick Start Workflow

  1. Open Krita and create or open any document.
  2. Open the docker: Settings → Dockers → 3D Layer.
  3. Click 📦 Primitives ▾ → Asaro Head to load the built-in reference model, or click 📁 Import 3D to open any .obj, .stl, .glb, or .gltf model.
  4. Select a preset from the PRESETS dropdown (e.g. Default Studio 3/4, Dramatic Fisheye 180°, or 5-Point Comic) to automatically configure the view.
  5. Interact with the 3D viewport (Left-drag to Orbit, Alt+Left or Middle-drag to Pan, Ctrl+Left or Right-drag to Zoom, Scroll to adjust FOV).
  6. With Live Sync checked, the 3D model and perspective grids render continuously to your active Krita canvas layer!

🎮 Viewport Navigation & Shortcuts

Action Mouse / Keyboard Shortcut Description
Orbit Camera Left Click + Drag Rotate the camera around the target object.
Pan Camera Middle Click or Alt + Left Drag Translate the camera view horizontally and vertically.
Zoom Distance Right Click + Drag or Ctrl + Left Drag Move the camera closer to or further from the target.
Field of View (FOV) Mouse Scroll Wheel Change lens focal length / perspective distortion angle.
Adjust Light Shift + Left Drag Rotate directional studio key light azimuth and elevation.
Frame / Reset Double Click Viewport Comfortably frame the 3D model in the center of the viewport.
Resize Viewport Drag Separator Handle Freely expand or contract the viewport height right below the preview.

⚙️ Presets & Configuration Storage

Where are parameters saved?

Parameters and custom configurations are saved into JSON files in Krita's standard user data directory:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\krita\krita_3d_layer_presets.json
    (Usually C:\Users\<Username>\AppData\Roaming\krita\krita_3d_layer_presets.json)
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/krita/krita_3d_layer_presets.json
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/krita/krita_3d_layer_presets.json

Using the Preset System:

  • Automatic Loading: Simply selecting any preset in the PRESETS dropdown immediately applies all camera angles (yaw, pitch, roll, tilt), distance, FOV, projection mode, fisheye curvature, grid planes, horizon visibility, studio lighting, and shading style.
  • Save Custom Preset (💾 Save...): Click this button to save your current viewport setup under any custom name. It will be added to the dropdown and persisted permanently in your presets JSON file.
  • Delete Custom Preset (🗑): Removes the selected custom preset from the JSON file. (Built-in presets are protected).

📐 3D Perspective Grids & Horizon Line

  • Ground Floor Grid: Accurate 3D square tiles spanning the ground plane with full crossing lines across all quadrants.
  • Ceiling Grid (Above): Renders a matching ceiling grid plane at an adjustable height above ground (ideal for rooms and architecture).
  • Eye-Level Horizon Line: Guaranteed visible horizon guide. In linear perspective it draws a straight horizon line; in Fisheye and 5-Point Curvilinear modes, it dynamically curves into a smooth lens arc as the camera tilts!
  • Extend to Horizon: Extends primary grid lines deep towards vanishing points.
  • Height Poles: Vertical perspective guide markers connecting ground to ceiling.

🐟 Fisheye & Curvilinear Projections

  • Fisheye Lenses: Supports Stereographic, Equisolid, Orthographic, and Equidistant spherical lens mapping formulas.
  • Curvature Strength: Control the intensity of barrel distortion — dial up to 150%–200% for intense dynamic manga and comic background curvature.
  • Circular Vignette: Toggle circular peephole lens aperture mask.
  • Lens Zoom: Zoom into the fisheye sphere to fill the rectangular frame.

🎨 Shading Styles & Silhouette Contours

  • Shaded + Wireframe: Soft directional Lambertian shading with ink wireframe edges.
  • Shaded: Clean planar shading, perfect for value studies.
  • Wireframe: Transparent background wireframe with backface culling for line art tracing.
  • Quad Wire (Hide Diagonals): Automatically hides internal triangulation diagonals on flat surfaces and quads for clean, Blender-style quad wireframe meshes.
  • Silhouette: Flat solid silhouette mask for shape blocking.
  • Contour Outline: Adjustable outer silhouette contour ink stroke.

🛠️ Ground Perspective Calibrator

Align the 3D camera to an existing 2D drawing or photo:

  1. Click 📐 Calibrate Ground... in the docker.
  2. Drag the 4 corner pins to match any rectangular feature in your sketch or reference photo (e.g. a tabletop, floor tile, or rug).
  3. Click Apply to Camera & Grid to automatically compute camera focal length (FOV), pitch, yaw, roll, and eye height!
